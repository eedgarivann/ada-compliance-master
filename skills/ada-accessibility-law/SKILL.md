# ADA accessibility law

Verified 2026-11-15. General, cited, dated; never legal advice. Audit: **ada-accessibility-audit**; log/statement: **ada-accessibility-deliverables**.

## Rules
- Answer law questions in a few sentences, 1–2 citations; full picture only when asked.
- Cite primary sources; label secondary (firm blogs, trackers); give source and verification dates. Web-search **volatile facts** (end) before stating.
- Mark settled vs disputed. Proposed rules, ANPRMs, bills are not law.
- Never promise compliance, immunity or outcomes.
- Don't request privileged documents. A shared demand letter may be read to list alleged barriers; don't assess merit; counsel should see it.

## US federal
**ADA Title III**
- *Settled:* 42 U.S.C. §12182. *DOJ position (non-binding):* it covers web content of businesses open to the public ("Guidance on Web Accessibility and the ADA", 18 Mar 2022; not among 11 documents withdrawn 19 Mar 2025).
- *Settled:* no Title III web regulation; web ANPRM withdrawn 26 Dec 2017 (82 FR 60932). WCAG 2.1 AA = usual benchmark in settlements, decrees, complaints.
- *Disputed:* website as "place of public accommodation". Nexus: 3d, 6th, 9th Cir. (*Robles v. Domino's Pizza*, 913 F.3d 898 (9th Cir. 2019), cert. denied; *Grossinger v. GoDaddy.com*, E.D. Pa. 7 Aug 2026, web-only dismissed). Broader: *Carparts*, 37 F.3d 12 (1st Cir. 1994); 7th Cir. dicta (*Doe*, 179 F.3d 557); 2d Cir. undecided, some S.D.N.Y./E.D.N.Y. judges. 11th Cir.: *Gil v. Winn-Dixie*, 993 F.3d 1266 (2021), **vacated as moot**, 21 F.4th 775; not binding. Foundational: *NFB v. Target*, 452 F. Supp. 2d 946 (N.D. Cal. 2006), settled 2008.
- *Disputed:* tester standing. *Acheson Hotels v. Laufer*, 601 U.S. 1 (2023), moot; split: 1st, 4th, 11th yes; 2nd, 5th, 10th no. *Calcano v. Swarovski*, 36 F.4th 68 (2d Cir. 2022): plausible intent to return; S.D.N.Y. still dismisses (*Fernandez v. Buffalo Billfold Co.*, Aug 2026). Secondary: NY filings shifting to NY/NJ state courts.
- *Remedies:* private = injunction + fees, no damages (§12188); DOJ civil penalties; damages via state claims.

**Title II (state/local gov)**: DOJ rule 89 FR 31320 (24 Apr 2024; 28 CFR 35 subpart H): web + mobile apps meet **WCAG 2.1 AA**; exceptions (28 CFR 35.201: archived; preexisting docs unless still used to apply for, access or join services (b); third-party posts; secured individual docs; old social posts); fundamental-alteration/undue-burden limits. Interim final rule 91 FR 20902 (eff. 20 Apr 2026): **26 Apr 2027** (population ≥50,000), **26 Apr 2028** (smaller, special districts) (28 CFR 35.200). Watch: DOJ may propose "less burdensome" changes (secondary); in force as amended.

**Section 504** (federal funds; many nonprofits, hospitals, universities, housing providers; regardless of Title III; each funding agency has its own regs). HHS 45 CFR 84.84 adopts WCAG 2.1 AA; extension (FR 11 May 2026): **11 May 2027** (15+ employees), **10 May 2028** (<15).

**Housing.** *Settled:* FHA bars discrimination in sale/rental and related services (42 U.S.C. §3604(f)(1)–(2)); refusing needed **reasonable accommodations** in rules, policies, practices, services is discrimination (§3604(f)(3)(B)); coverage fact-dependent; design/construction (§3604(f)(3)(C)) is physical. Enforcement: HUD, DOJ, private suits. HUD 504 rules **24 CFR part 8** cover HUD-assisted recipients (§8.1–8.4, incl. contracted/licensed); §8.6 **effective communication**: auxiliary aids, primary consideration to requested aid, accessible info, limited by fundamental alteration/undue burden (alternatives still required). *Not established:* no WCAG version or web deadline for recipients; HUD's Digital Accessibility Policy (stricter of 508 and WCAG 2.2 AA) covers HUD's own ICT only; 2023 ANPRM (88 FR 24938, 25 Apr 2023) not law. *Practice:* barriers on housing sites/portals can support FHA/504 complaints; Title III may reach rental offices and public amenities, not dwellings; beyond that, housing counsel.

**Section 508:** federal agency ICT (29 U.S.C. §794d); 36 CFR 1194 incorporates **WCAG 2.0 AA**; vendors asked for 508 ACRs; not private sites.
**FTC:** accessibility claims are advertising; accessiBe final order 21 Apr 2025 ($1M, 20 years). Don't claim "fully ADA compliant" without evidence.

## Outside the US
- **EAA** (Directive (EU) 2019/882): from **28 Jun 2025**; e-commerce, consumer banking, e-books, transport ticketing, e-communications; reaches non-EU sellers to EU consumers. **Microenterprises** (<10 staff and ≤€2M) exempt for services. Presumption via **EN 301 549** (≈WCAG 2.1 AA). Enforcement varies by state.
- **AODA** (O. Reg. 191/11): orgs **50+ employees** + public bodies: **WCAG 2.0 AA** (except live captions, prerecorded AD) since 1 Jan 2021; reporting.
- **Accessible Canada Act** (federally regulated): **CAN/ASC-EN 301 549:2024**. Pages: public sector **5 Dec 2027**, large/medium (100+) **5 Dec 2028**. Apps, docs, procurement: public sector and large (500+) **5 Dec 2028**. Statements: public sector (web) **5 Dec 2027**, +apps/docs and large **5 Dec 2028**. Training (all three) **5 Dec 2027**. Penalties.
- UK Equality Act 2010, Australia DDA, Israel: mention, research first.

## How claims proceed (general only)
Demand letter (often tester, WCAG 2.1 AA; often skipped) → complaint, federal (injunction, fees) and/or state (damages: Unruh, NYCHRL) → early motions (standing, mootness, coverage); most resolve early → settlement/decree (2.1 AA, policy, training, audits, fees). Partial fixes invite repeat suits. Never advise response, settlement, defense or claim value.

## Demand letter path
In chat: steps 1–3 in short bullets (counsel first), rest on request.
1. Don't ignore; **contact qualified counsel now** (digital-accessibility defense, that jurisdiction). 2. Preserve dated screenshots, HTML, scans, deploy history; don't delete/hide pages; ask counsel about preservation. 3. Fix real barriers on cited pages via the ladder (counsel may time it). 4. Log them in remediation log. 5. Keep counsel informed; they may commission audits. 6. You help with barriers, WCAG mapping, fixes and documentation — not strategy, merits, settlement or plaintiff contact.
