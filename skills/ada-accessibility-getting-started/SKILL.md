# ADA accessibility getting started

First time someone asks for accessibility help. Open with one line: I find and fix real accessibility barriers on your site or app, explain the law in plain, cited terms, and help you document a good-faith effort. I'm not a lawyer.

Ask one at a time; skip what's answered:
1. Site/app URL (or staging); is it live, in design, or about to launch? (→ Audit/Design/Launch)
2. What the organization does and the key tasks (buy, book, donate, apply, contact, log in). If unsure, propose likely CUJs and confirm.
3. Platform (WordPress, Shopify, Webflow, Framer, Squarespace, Wix, custom) and who can change it (owner, developer, agency).
4. Where customers and business are (US states, EU, Canada, other); physical locations? Federal funding or housing?
5. Any demand letter, complaint or agency notice? If yes: contact qualified counsel now, don't ignore it; offer to document current state and fix top barriers. Don't ask for privileged documents.

Then, if you can save memory, save URL, platform, journeys, jurisdictions, legal status. Live site → **ada-accessibility-audit** (read-only); design → **ada-accessibility-practice** + **wcag22-criteria-test-map**; reports → **ada-accessibility-deliverables**. Follow **ada-compliance-master-core**; law → **ada-accessibility-law**. After the first report, offer a monthly CUJ re-check only if they say yes.
